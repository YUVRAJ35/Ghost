EXPANDED SERVER RULES & CODE OF CONDUCT

Please read all server rules carefully. Ignorance of rules is not an excuse for violations.

1. CHAT & COMMUNITY RULES

  • 1.1 Respect All Players: Severe toxicity, hate speech, racism, sexism, or discrimination will result in instant permanent muted/banned status.
  • 1.2 No Spamming: Fast repetitive messages, all-caps spam, or character floods are strictly blocked.
  • 1.3 No Advertising: Promoting other Minecraft servers, Discord servers, or commercial links is forbidden.
  • 1.4 Staff Impersonation: Falsely claiming to be an Owner, Admin, or Moderator will lead to an immediate network ban.

2. GAMEPLAY & FAIR PLAY

  • 2.1 Zero Tolerance Hacking: Use of X-Ray, Fly, KillAura, Speed, Auto-clickers (>15 CPS), or modified clients is permanently bannable.
  • 2.2 Exploits & Duping: Exploiting server bugs, item duplication, or map glitches will result in complete account wipes and IP bans.
  • 2.3 Allowed Mods: OptiFine, Sodium, Minimaps (without player radar), and purely aesthetic HUD mods are allowed.
  • 2.4 AFK Farms: Auto-clicker AFK farming is prohibited. Passive mob farms within reasonable chunk limits are allowed.

3. CLAIMS & GRIEF PREVENTION

  • 3.1 Land Claim Safety: Players must protect their builds using the claim system. Griefing unclaimed builds is frowned upon, but claimed land is 100% protected.
  • 3.2 Claim Surround Griefing: Lava-casting, water-grieving, or building obtrusive structures right outside someone's claim boundary is strictly punishable.
  • 3.3 Portal Trapping: Trapping Nether or End portals to permanently kill players is prohibited.

4. ECONOMY & TRADING RULES

  • 4.1 Scamming Policy: In-game currency or item scamming during official auctions or direct trades is punishable by item rollback.
  • 4.2 Real-Money Trading (RMT): Trading in-game cash or items for real money outside the official server store is strictly forbidden.
  • 4.3 Chargeback Protection: Fraudulent billing chargebacks trigger automated IP bans across the entire OderisMC network.
